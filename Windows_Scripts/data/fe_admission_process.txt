FIRST YEAR (F.Y.) B.TECH ADMISSION PROCESS
Academic Year: 2025–26
PVG’s College of Engineering, Technology and Management, Pune

This document outlines the complete admission procedure, fee payment process, reporting workflow, and required documentation for First Year B.Tech admissions.

------------------------------------------------------------

PRE-REPORTING CHECKLIST

Before reporting to the college for admission, students must ensure the following:

• Scanned copies of all original documents in .jpeg/.jpg/.tiff/.bmp format  
  (Maximum size: 256 KB per document)

• Provision for payment of applicable college fees

• Minimum 3 self-attested xerox sets of all required documents

• Generation of Academic Bank of Credits (ABC ID)  
  OR Annual Performance Appraisal Report (APAR ID)

------------------------------------------------------------

ADMISSION PROCESS (OFFLINE – COLLEGE LEVEL)

Step-wise procedure after CAP allotment:

1. Data scrutiny and document verification  
2. Fee payment challan generation as per student category  
3. Payment of college fees via:
   • Cash  
   • Demand Draft (DD)  
   • UPI (GPay / PhonePe / BHIM)

   Note: Credit card / Debit card / Cheque payments are NOT permitted.

4. Generation of:
   • Admission Form  
   • College Registration Number (CRN)  
   • University Eligibility Form

5. Submission of 2 self-attested xerox sets of documents  
6. Original document verification at CET Cell portal  
7. Final admission confirmation at CET Cell portal  

Students receive CET Cell admission receipt — mandatory for valid admission.
