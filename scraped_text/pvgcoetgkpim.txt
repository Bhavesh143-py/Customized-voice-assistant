ABOUT THE INSTITUTE
Pune Vidhyarthi Griha’s College of Engineering, Technology and Management (PVGCOET&M), Pune

PVGCOET&M is a reputed engineering institution established in 1985 under the Pune Vidhyarthi Griha trust. The institute is recognized for its academic infrastructure, industry-oriented programs, and research initiatives.

------------------------------------------------------------

ACCREDITATION & AFFILIATION

• Approved by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)  
• Permanently affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU)  
• Accredited by NAAC with ‘A’ Grade (Second Cycle, Revised ICT Framework)  
• Approved and rated ‘A’ Grade by Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Maharashtra  

These recognitions validate the institute’s academic quality, governance, and infrastructure standards.

------------------------------------------------------------

UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS (UG)

The institute offers Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) programs in:

• Printing and Packaging Technology  
• Mechanical Engineering  
• Electrical Engineering  
• Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering  
• Information Technology  
• Computer Engineering  
• Artificial Intelligence and Data Science  
• Master of Business Administration (MBA)  

These programs are aligned with industry demand and emerging technology domains.

------------------------------------------------------------

POSTGRADUATE PROGRAMS (PG)

Postgraduate specializations include:

• Electrical Engineering  
• Printing and Packaging Technology  
• Master of Business Administration (MBA)  

These programs emphasize advanced technical training and managerial development.

------------------------------------------------------------

DOCTORAL (PhD) RESEARCH PROGRAMS

The institute offers PhD research opportunities in:

• Electrical Engineering  
• Mechanical Engineering  
• Printing and Packaging Technology  

Doctoral programs focus on applied research, innovation, and industry collaboration.

CAMPUS INFRASTRUCTURE

The campus spans modern academic and residential facilities:

• Total land area: 8 Acres  
• Built-up area: 25,630 sq. meters  
• 800+ computers for academic use  
• Centralized computing infrastructure  
• Distributed campus internet network  

This infrastructure supports academic delivery, research, and digital learning.

ACADEMIC & STUDENT FACILITIES

The institute provides multiple support facilities:
