#### _GENERAL RULES:_
1. Admission given is only for one academic year.
2. Allotted room will not be changed / swapped without permission of the Rector. The Rector reserves the right to change the room of the student.
3. The student should bring his own mattress (cot size 2 1/2feet by 6 1/4feet), bed sheet, blanket etc.
4. Hot water will be provided only during 6.00 A.M. to 9.00 A.M.
5. Students must return to the hostel and be in their rooms before 10.00 P.M. on Monday to Saturday and 10.30 P.M on Sunday.
6. No student will be allowed to enter in the hostel beyond the permissible time.
7. It is obligatory on the part of the student seeking admission in the hostel for the first time to introduce his parents and local guardians to the
8. Friends, parents and guests are not allowed to stay in the hostel.
9. Students should not use any type of electric heating appliance, Iron as well as Radio, Transistor in their rooms. The student will be fined if such appliances are found in the room and also the said appliances will be confiscated and will not be returned.
10. Students are advised not to possess excessive cash, valuables, costly electronic gadgets etc in the hostel room. The hostel authorities will not be responsible for any loss, damage or theft of personal things, cash or any other belongings (mobile phones, laptops etc.)
11. Parties or birthday celebrations inside the hostel premises are strictly prohibited.
12. Playing with dry/ wet colours or with plain water, using crackers insides hostel premises are not allowed.
13. Student must put off the lights, Fan and close the water taps before they lock their room. All students in the room will be penalised if lights, fans are seen ‘ON’ in locked room.
14. No change in the position of the furniture is allowed in the room. The student will be held responsible and will be fined if he damages any furniture or fixture in his room or of the hostel premises.
15. Bringing lunch/dinner tiffin or breakfast or any kind of food/beverages inside the room is strictly prohibited.
16. Students should keep their rooms clean, neat, and tidy and maintain hygienic conditions so as to keep diseases away. Make use of dustbins.
17. In case of illness or any other emergency, the Students should immediately report to the Rector for further appropriate action. All expenses related to medical treatment will be borne by the student.
18. Students should maintain discipline and peaceful atmosphere in the hostel as well as in mess and reading hall. Making noise or talking loudly in the hostel is forbidden.
19. Each student will be given hostel Identity Card. This card must be shown to the watchman/rector if asked for.
20. Students should not play any outdoor games such as Cricket, Volleyball in the hostel premises.
21. Students should not get involved in any kind of **Ragging Activity** otherwise strict action at hostel and college level will be taken.
22. Smoking, Chewing tobacco or pan, spitting as well as consuming alcoholic drinks is strictly prohibited. In case of violation of the rule in hostel or campus, he will be rusticated from hostel immediately. In that case hostel fee will not be refunded.
23. Misbehavior with security guards/watchman/ mess staff/Waiters/ or any other official of the hostel will be treated as an act of indiscipline.
24. Indulgence in any kind of undesirable activity will be treated as breach of discipline. Such students will be fined and may also be expelled from the hostel immediately and the fees will be forfeited.
25. Any suggestion regarding the improvement in hostel facilities should be made to the hostel rector in writing.
26. Photos or Videos of rooms and other facilities should not be posted on social media without permission of the Hostel Rector.
**ATTENDANCE RULES:**
* Students taking leave should clearly mention address where they are going, telephone numbers, dates and other required information in the leave form.
* Students must vacate the hostel rooms immediately the next day after completion of final examination.
**MESS RULES:**
* Mess facility is optional.
* Only pure vegetarian food will be provided.
* Students should not bring any kind of outside food in the hostel room. Food/beverages should not be brought in the hostel room.
* Mess will be closed on every Sunday evening.
**REFUND RULES:**
* If the hostel admission is cancelled before the last CAP round of allotment / admission to the college, 10% fees will be deducted and the remaining fees will be refunded.
* If any students wants to cancel his hostel admission after the admission process of the college is over, **No fees will be refunded.**
