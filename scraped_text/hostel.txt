GENERAL HOSTEL RULES

1. Hostel admission is valid for one academic year only.  
2. Room swapping is not allowed without Rector approval.  
3. Students must bring personal bedding items.  
4. Furniture rearrangement is prohibited.  
5. Guests or visitors are not allowed to stay.  
6. Electric heating appliances are prohibited.  
7. Hostel authorities are not responsible for loss of valuables.  
8. Celebrations, parties, or crackers are strictly prohibited.  
9. Students must maintain cleanliness and hygiene.  
10. Medical emergencies must be reported to the Rector.  
11. Noise, indiscipline, or misconduct is punishable.  
12. Ragging is strictly prohibited under institutional rules.  
13. Smoking, alcohol, and tobacco use are banned.  
14. Misbehavior with hostel staff invites disciplinary action.  
15. Social media posting of hostel premises without permission is prohibited.

ATTENDANCE & LEAVE RULES

• Students must submit leave applications in advance.  
• Address, contact number, and duration must be provided.  
• Parents/guardians must be informed.  
• Students must vacate rooms after final examinations.

MESS RULES

• Mess enrollment is optional.  
• Only vegetarian food is served.  
• Outside food is not permitted in rooms.  
• Mess remains closed on Sunday evenings.

HOSTEL REFUND POLICY

• Cancellation after admission completion → No refund.
