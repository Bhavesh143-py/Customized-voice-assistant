CAMPUS & INFRASTRUCTURE OVERVIEW
PVG’s College of Engineering, Technology and Management (PVGCOET&M), Pune

Pune Vidhyarthi Griha’s College of Engineering, Technology and Management was established in 1985 and is affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University. The institute is accredited with ‘A’ Grade by NAAC and is recognized for its academic infrastructure and student support facilities.

------------------------------------------------------------

CAMPUS FACILITIES

The campus provides a wide range of academic and residential support amenities:

• Distributed high-speed internet connectivity  
• Open-air theater for cultural programs and events  
• Banking facility within campus proximity  
• Boys hostel with mess and canteen services  
• Telephone booth facility  
• Xerox and reprography services  
• Uninterrupted power supply with 260 KVA backup  

These facilities support both academic and extracurricular student activities.

------------------------------------------------------------

INFRASTRUCTURE DETAILS

The institute’s physical and digital infrastructure includes:

• Total land area: 8 Acres  
• Built-up area: 25,630 sq. meters  
• 800+ computers for academic use  
• Centralized computing facility  
• Distributed networking infrastructure  
• Modern laboratories and workshops  

This infrastructure enables practical learning, research work, and project development.

------------------------------------------------------------

STUDENT AMENITIES

To support student life on campus, the following amenities are available:

• Gymnasium and fitness facilities  
• Playground and sports infrastructure  
• Vehicle parking for students and staff  
• Filtered drinking water facilities  

These amenities promote physical well-being and campus convenience.

------------------------------------------------------------

HOSTEL & RESIDENTIAL SUPPORT

Residential accommodation includes:

• Boys hostel (approx. capacity: 135 students)  
• Separate girls hostel facility  
• Mess and dining services  

Hostel allotment is subject to institutional policies and availability.

------------------------------------------------------------

POWER & UTILITIES

• 260 kVA backup generator ensures uninterrupted electricity supply  
• Continuous water and utility services are maintained across campus
